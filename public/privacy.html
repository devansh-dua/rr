SITE INFORMATION

Privacy

This page describes the dashboard as delivered. Review and update it if hosting, analytics, forms, or data collection are added.

Information this dashboard handles

The dashboard is a static, client-side project. It does not ask visitors to create an account, submit personal information, or upload files. Search, sorting, and weight adjustments run in the visitor’s browser and are not sent to the project owner by the dashboard.

Hosting and server logs

When hosted publicly, the hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP address, browser details, and requested pages in its operational logs. The provider’s own privacy terms govern that processing. Confirm the actual host and its log retention practices before public launch.

External services

The dashboard embeds a YouTube video in its opening pitch popup. Loading this page can connect your browser to YouTube and share technical request information with Google. YouTube may use cookies or similar technologies according to its own privacy terms. The video does not autoplay. The player loads when the dashboard opens. A browser content blocker may prevent the YouTube connection. The dashboard does not include analytics or advertising.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site operator using the contact details published with the deployed site. No contact form is provided here.
